Why users is split across two migrations

Framework identity vs. LMS domain. Root 0001_…_create_users_table is pristine Laravel — name, email, password. Auth-module 120000_add_manager_fields… adds LMS citizenship: manager_id (authority tree, RULE-002) and approval_status (instructor gate, RULE-010).

Root stays stock (upgrades, Filament, familiar ground) · domain diffs live with their owner in Modules/Auth · migration order narrates boot order. One combined migration would weld LMS concepts into framework scaffolding.
